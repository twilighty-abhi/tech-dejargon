Think of an API like a waiter. You don't go to the kitchen to make your own food. You tell the waiter, they take the request to the kitchen, and bring back what you asked for.

APIs work the same way. They let different apps and systems ask each other for information or actions without needing to know what's happening behind the scenes.

For Your Impact Team

Instead of manually downloading CSVs and copying donor lists, an API lets your website talk directly to your CRM automatically.
